These are the three pages most useful away from a screen: your rights, how to write a request, and what to do after a denial. Each prints in black on white, with the web address spelled out after every link, including links to other pages of this manual. Each runs to more than one page; printing double-sided saves paper. Use the button beside a title to print that sheet alone.

Without JavaScript, or from a phone's share menu, use your browser's Print command: this page then prints all three sheets, each starting on a new page.
