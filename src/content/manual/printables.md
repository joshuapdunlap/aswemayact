These are the four pages most useful away from a screen: your rights, how to write a request, what to do after a denial, and the rules a public meeting has to follow, to take to one. Each prints in black on white, with the web address spelled out after every link, including links to other pages of this manual. Each runs to more than one page; printing double-sided saves paper. Use the button beside a title to print that sheet alone.

Without JavaScript, or from a phone's share menu, use your browser's Print command: this page then prints all four sheets, each starting on a new page.
