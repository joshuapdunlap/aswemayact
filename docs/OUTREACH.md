# Outreach: who puts the site and the printables in front of Arkansans

Status: researched 2026-10-15; nobody has been contacted except where a row says so.
Organizations and their published contact routes only. Before writing, re-open the contact
page: these change.

Lead with something to use: the printables (`/manual/printables/`) and the letter builder.
Offer paper where an organization has a rack or a board.

## Order of approach

1. **SPJ Arkansas Pro Chapter.** The best single channel to working journalists; it has a
   standing FOI Committee and gives the McCord FOIA Award. <https://arkansasspj.org/officers-contact/>.
   A review request is drafted in Gmail.
2. **League of Women Voters.** The state league (<https://lwvar.org/contact/>, newsletter
   <https://lwvar.org/newsletter/>), and the Faulkner County (<https://lwvarfc.org/contact/>)
   and Washington County (<https://www.lwvarwc.org/contact>) leagues, whose Observer Corps
   already attend public meetings. They are the natural partners for the
   [Act 505 audit](act-505-audit/METHOD.md). Three new leagues (Upper Delta, Garland County,
   Jonesboro) opened in July 2026; reach them through the state league.
3. **Libraries, statewide.** The Arkansas State Library's Library Development unit
   (<https://library.arkansas.gov/services/library-consulting/public-library-consulting/>),
   which already lists the AG's FOI handbook for public libraries; and the Arkansas Library
   Association (contact <https://arlib.org/form.php?form_id=13>; the ARKLIB-L list is open
   to allies; *Arkansas Libraries* takes submissions by May 1 and Nov. 1).
4. **Libraries with a published route for handouts.**
   - Craighead County Jonesboro Public Library: literature rack and boards, 30 days.
     <https://www.libraryinjonesboro.org/about-us/contact-information.php>
   - Bentonville Public Library: community information board.
     <https://bentonvillelibrary.org/about-us/connect-with-us>
   - Fayetteville Public Library: display priority for government and nonprofit notices.
     <https://www.faylib.org/contact-8267>
   - Central Arkansas Library System: civic displays (policy 402) and free-publication racks
     (policy 405). <https://cals.org/contact/>. Also ask the Encyclopedia of Arkansas to add the
     site to its FOIA entry's further reading: <https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/contact-us/>.
   - Faulkner County Library: first-come racks for free informational material. `fcl@fcl.org`
   - Springdale Public Library: nonprofit display space through its programming coordinator;
     its service area overlaps the Marshallese and Spanish-speaking communities.
     <https://springdalelibrary.org/contact/>
   - Fort Smith (pamphlets with permission), Rogers, Garland County, Pine Bluff: ask.
5. **Journalism programs and student papers** (teaching use, and a story):
   - UA Fayetteville School of Journalism and Strategic Media, <https://journalism.uark.edu/about-the-school>; *The Arkansas Traveler*, <https://uatrav.com/>
   - UA Little Rock School of Mass Communication, <https://ualr.edu/masscomm/contact/>; *The Forum*
   - Arkansas State School of Media and Journalism; *The Herald*, <https://astatetheherald.com/>
   - UCA School of Communication, <https://uca.edu/communication/>; *The Echo*, <https://ucanews.live/contact/>
   - Arkansas College Media Association (every campus paper and adviser), <https://acma1929.wordpress.com/>
6. **Wider audiences.**
   - Arkansas Advocate: a signed 750-word commentary at launch, and its stories may be
     republished. <https://arkansasadvocate.com/commentary-submission-guidelines/>
   - Adult Learning Alliance of Arkansas (literacy councils in 50+ counties): plain-language
     handouts. <https://arkansasliteracy.org/contact-us/>
   - Arkansas Public Policy Panel (organizers who use records requests). <https://arpanel.org/contact>
   - Arkansas Law Help has no public-records topic; worth a pitch. <https://www.arlawhelp.org/>
   - Humanities Arkansas could fund a library workshop series through a partner (eligibility
     unverified). <https://humanitiesar.org/about-us/contact/>
   - Arkansas Press Association: the free weekly *Arkansas Publisher Weekly* reaches member
     papers; a paid release service ($200 statewide) exists. <https://arkansaspress.org/contact-us/>

**Low priority:** the Association of Arkansas Counties and the Arkansas Municipal League
write for officials and show no route for outside material. No Code for America brigade is
active in Arkansas (the program ended in 2023).

## Translation partners (Release 2)

See [translation/BRIEF.md](translation/BRIEF.md) for the work. Four of these share one
building, 614 E. Emma Ave., Springdale, which makes a single review session practical.

**Marshallese**

- Arkansas Coalition of Marshallese (ACOM): offers translation and interpreting.
  <https://www.arkansasmarshallese.org/contact-1>
- Marshallese Educational Initiative (MEI): translation on a fee basis, including government
  forms. <https://www.mei.ngo/services-7>
- Chikin Meļeļe News: translation to and from Marshallese; past clients include the state
  Department of Health. <https://chikinmelele.com/translation-media-and-sponsored-content/>
- RMI Consulate General, Springdale: a credible reviewer or partner for sharing the sheet
  (contact via <https://www.rmiembassyus.org/contact>).
- UAMS Institute for Community Health Innovation, Springdale: introductions to community
  reviewers. <https://communityhealth.uams.edu/work-with-us/>

Plan: one of ACOM or MEI translates, the other (or Chikin Meļeļe) reviews. **Cost
benchmark:** on the state Department of Human Services' 2024 Marshallese solicitation
(710-25-040), ACOM bid $0.32 a word to translate and $0.10 to review, MEI $0.35 and $0.15.
At those rates a 350-word sheet is roughly $150 to $175 translated and reviewed. Those are
government volume prices; ask for a quote.

Glossary help: the RMI Judiciary's *Marshallese Legal Glossary* (2022),
<https://rmicourts.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Kajin-Jikin-Ekajet-ilo-Tukaduin-Marshallese-Legal-Glossary-in-Official-Spelling-6.15.2022.pdf>,
and the Arkansas courts' interpreter brochure in Marshallese,
<https://arcourts.gov/sites/default/files/IntroBrochure%20Marshallese_0.pdf>.

**Spanish**

- Hispanic Community Services / El Centro Hispano (Jonesboro): document translation.
  <https://www.centrohispanoarkansas.com/contact/>
- ATA members in Arkansas (directory filter), for professional translation.
  <https://www.atanet.org/member-directory/>
- Community review: Arkansas United (<https://arkansasunited.org/en/contactus>), the
  Hispanic Women's Organization of Arkansas (<https://www.hwoa.org/>), and AID Arkansas,
  whose lawyers work in Spanish and can check the legal terms (<https://aidarkansas.com/>).
