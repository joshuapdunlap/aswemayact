## Who does it

Candidate translators and reviewers, with a cost benchmark, are in
[../OUTREACH.md](../OUTREACH.md#translation-partners-release-2).

- **One paid translator per language**, working from the frozen English. Marshallese is the
  harder hire: few professional translators work in it, and fewer with legal vocabulary.
  Start with the Marshallese community organizations in Northwest Arkansas for referrals.
- **One community reviewer per language**, paid, who did not translate the sheet. The
  reviewer reads it as a neighbor would and marks anything that reads as stiff, wrong or
  unclear. The question is "would my aunt understand this?", not "is it word for word?".
- **A back-translation** of each final sheet into English by a third person, which the
  editor checks against the source for legal meaning. This is where a mistranslated
  deadline or right gets caught.

No machine translation in the deliverable. A translator may use whatever tools they like,
but the sheet is theirs and the reviewer's.

## Rules for the translation

1. **Keep every citation exactly as written**: `Ark. Code Ann. § 25-19-105(a)(3)`, act
   numbers, dates. Do not translate or reformat them.
2. **Keep the web addresses in English** (`aswemayact.org/manual/write/`). The site is in
   English; say so on the sheet (see the line at the foot of each source text).
3. **Plain register.** Write as you would speak to a neighbor at a kitchen table, not as a
   court would write. Short sentences.
4. **Glossary terms** (below) get one rendering each, used the same way on both sheets.
   Where a term has no natural equivalent, keep the English word in parentheses after the
   first use: in Spanish, *custodio de los registros (custodian)*.
5. **Numbers and money** stay as numbers: `$25`, `3 working days`.
6. **Length.** Each sheet must still fit one side of US Letter at 11 pt. If it does not,
   tell us; we will cut the English, not squeeze the type.

## Glossary

| ------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| public record | anything a public office keeps about public business, in any form | registro público | translator to propose |

## Open questions for the editor before freezing

- **Citizenship.** The right belongs to Arkansas *citizens*. Many readers of a Marshallese
  sheet are lawfully present under the Compact of Free Association but are not U.S.
  citizens. The full page *Who may request* covers what counts and the proxy route. The
  one-pager points there; confirm that is enough, or add a sentence.
- **Language of the request.** The Act says nothing about the language a request is written
  in. The sheets do not say a request may be written in Spanish or Marshallese; they point
  to the English letter builder. Decide whether to say more, and on what authority.
- **Print files.** The one-pagers are not yet pages on the site. Decide whether they live at
  `/manual/printables/` beside the English sheets, or as PDFs.

## Delivery

Translator returns plain text (or .docx) with the frozen source date at the top. Reviewer's
notes and the back-translation go in this folder, one file each, named
`<sheet>.<lang>.review.md` and `<sheet>.<lang>.back.md`.
